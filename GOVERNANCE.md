# Governance

CyberLink Shield is currently in the **bootstrap stage**. Until a maintainer group is formally established, the repository owner acts as project steward.

## Proposed model
- Maintainers review and merge contributions.
- Security-sensitive changes require at least one additional reviewer when the team permits.
- Releases require a changelog entry, passing CI, and signed artifacts when release signing is configured.
- Changes to privacy defaults, telemetry, blocking policy, or feed trust require explicit design review.
- Contributors retain copyright and license their contributions under Apache-2.0.

This document should be updated when a maintainer team and decision process are formally established.
